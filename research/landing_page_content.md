# Helium Landing Page Content Strategy

## 1. PROTOCOL (The Technical Core)
*   **Whitepaper**: "Helium: A Decentralized Protocol for Verifiable Machine Learning on Proof of Useful Work (PoUW)". Focus on the ZK-ML verification layer and the hardware-agnostic scheduling engine.
*   **GitHub**: Link to `helium-core` (Architecture specs), `helium-miner` (Node implementation), and `helium-sdk` (Python/Rust bindings for AI devs).
*   **Roadmap**:
    *   Q1 2026: Genesis Block & Proof of Useful Work v1.
    *   Q2 2026: Zero-Knowledge ML Attestation (ZK-ML) Mainnet.
    *   Q3 2026: Elastic GPU Orchestration for XXL Models.
    *   Q4 2026: Cross-Chain Settlement & DAO Governance.

## 2. NETWORK (The Infrastructure)
*   **Explorer**: Real-time dashboard showing "Recent Proofs", "Verified Jobs", and "Global TFLOPS".
*   **Nodes**: Detailed guide for "Compute Nodes" (GPU providers) and "Validation Nodes" (ZK-Proof verifiers). Hardware requirements (RTX 3090+ recommended).
*   **Stats**: Live metrics on "Cost Savings vs Cloud (AWS/GCP)", "Network Latency", and "Total GPU Supply".

## 3. COMMUNITY (The Human Element)
*   **Discord**: Join the "Helium Forge" for miners and the "Helium Lab" for AI researchers.
*   **Twitter/X**: Daily updates on network growth, partner integrations, and "Mining Rewards" highlights.
*   **Forum**: Technical governance discussions on "Helium Improvement Proposals (HIPs)" and "PoUW Algorithm Updates".

## 4. LEGAL (The Compliance)
*   **Terms**: "Helium Network Terms of Service". Focus on the decentralized nature of the protocol (v1.0-kuro).
*   **Privacy**: "Protocol Privacy Policy". Emphasis on Zero-Knowledge data handling and non-custodial node operations.
*   **Security**: "Security & Bug Bounty Program". Details on the "Proof-of-Verification" audit trail and smart contract safety.
