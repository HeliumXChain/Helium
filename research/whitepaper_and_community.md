# Helium Whitepaper Outline (Draft v0.1)

## 1. Abstract
A high-level overview of the Helium Protocol: a decentralized Layer 1 blockchain for verifiable AI compute powered by Proof of Useful Work (PoUW).

## 2. The Problem: Centralized AI Compute
*   High costs of AWS/GCP clusters.
*   Lack of transparency in model training.
*   Centralized gatekeeping of AI hardware (NVIDIA H100 shortages).

## 3. The Helium Solution: Sovereign Compute
*   Repurposing global GPU mining power for AI.
*   The shift from "Proof of Wasted Work" (Bitcoin) to "Proof of Useful Work".

## 4. Technical Architecture (L1)
*   **Proof of Useful Work (PoUW)**: How consensus is reached through training epochs.
*   **Verifiable ML (ZK-ML)**: Using Zero-Knowledge proofs to verify model improvement without exposing private weights.
*   **Hardware Attestation**: TPM 2.0 integration for secure GPU execution.

## 5. Tokenomics ($HLM)
*   Supply: 1.0B total.
*   Incentives for Node Operators and AI Clients.
*   Burn-and-Mint Equilibrium (BME) or similar sustainable model.

## 6. Roadmap & Governance
*   Research L1 -> Alpha Simulation -> Beta Mainnet.
*   Community-led governance (HIPs).
